Activities, Indicator and Outputs:
Objective 1: To Strengthen institutional and community systems for inclusive, gender-affirmative, and rights-based SRHR, HIV, and GBV response for key population including LGBTQI in selected districts with the aim of creating supportive and friendly environment for the Key Population in government health facilities and their outreach services
Objective 2: To enhance the capacity of health and protection service providers, including midwives and law enforcement personnel, to deliver survivor-centered, stigma-free, and gender-responsive care and referral support.
Objective 4:  To build community awareness and social accountability mechanisms to reduce stigma, discrimination, and violence, and promote access to health and protection services among key populations.
