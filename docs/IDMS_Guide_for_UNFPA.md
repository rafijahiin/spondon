# The IDMS Dashboard — A Plain Guide for UNFPA
### What each screen shows, where the numbers come from, and how to find your way around

> This guide is written for programme staff, not engineers. It explains the dashboard
> in everyday language. Wherever a chart shows a number, we tell you **which form on the
> ground it came from** — the same form name you'll see on the small grey **"Source"** tag
> in the corner of every chart.

---

## 1. What the system is, in four sentences

- Field staff collect information on their phones using simple digital forms (KoboToolbox).
- The moment they press submit, that information arrives in the online dashboard.
- For PHD and Bandhu, a manager checks and **approves** each submission before it counts.
- Once approved, it appears in the charts. CIPRB's monitoring data appears straight away.

So every number you see has travelled this path:

> **A health worker fills a form on a phone  →  it arrives instantly  →  it is approved  →  it shows on the dashboard.**

---

## 2. The three partners at a glance

| Partner | Who they are / what they do | Where to see their data |
|---|---|---|
| **PHD** | Reaches female sex workers (FSWs) in brothel communities with SRHR, HIV and GBV services | The **PHD** page |
| **Bandhu** | Reaches key populations with SRHR, HIV and GBV services through Drop-in Centres | The **Bandhu** page |
| **CIPRB** | Does not deliver services — it **monitors** fistula, maternal/newborn death reviews (MPDSR) and near-miss cases | The **CIPRB** page |

A simple way to remember it: **PHD and Bandhu put data in; CIPRB and UNFPA watch over everything.**

---

## 3. Who can see what

| You are… | You can see… |
|---|---|
| **UNFPA** | **Everything** — all three partners and every tracker |
| **CIPRB** | **Everything** (monitoring role) |
| A **PHD** manager | Only PHD's data, and only the approval screen |
| A **Bandhu** manager | Only Bandhu's data, and only the approval screen |
| **Field staff** | Nothing on the dashboard — they only use the phone forms |

UNFPA also has one special job: it gives the **final approval** for Bandhu's submissions (see §4).

---

## 4. How the numbers become "official"

The approval step is slightly different for each partner. This matters because **a number only
counts once it is fully approved.**

| Partner | Approval steps | What this means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **PHD** | Manager approves → done | A PHD number appears once PHD's manager approves it |
| **Bandhu** | Manager approves → **then UNFPA approves** → done | **A Bandhu number appears only after UNFPA gives the second approval.** If a Bandhu chart looks low, check whether items are still waiting for UNFPA in the Approvals screen |
| **CIPRB** | No approval needed | CIPRB's monitoring data appears as soon as it is submitted |

*(Registrations — the PHD and Bandhu "patient lists" — are accepted automatically; they don't need approval.)*

## 5. Finding your way around the site

The menu on the left takes you to these places:

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | A bird's-eye view of all three partners — totals, recent activity, and which centres reported today |
| **PHD** | Everything about PHD (see §6) |
| **Bandhu** | Everything about Bandhu (see §7) |
| **CIPRB** | Fistula, MPDSR death reviews, and near-miss — all on one page (see §8) |
| **Approvals** | The queue of submissions waiting to be approved (managers and UNFPA) |
| **Progress Tracker / Targets** | Each indicator's target vs. what's achieved so far |
| **Reports / Infographics** | Ready-made reports and shareable summary cards |

On any partner page you can switch the headline numbers between **"this month"** and **"total so far"**,
and on the CIPRB page you can filter by donor (**All / GAC / SIDA**) to see only that donor's districts.

## 6. The PHD page — what you see and where it comes from

**The forms PHD field staff use:**
- **PHD 1 — FSW Registration** — registers each woman once, with a unique ID. This is PHD's master list.
- **PHD 2 — Service Log** — the daily form for every service: clinic visits, HIV tests, counselling, referrals, group sessions, trainings, materials, GBV corners and stock.
- **No Reporting Today** — filed by a centre on a day it has nothing to report (so it isn't marked "silent").

**What's on the page:**

| What you see | What it means | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **5 headline cards** (FSWs reached, Wellness centres, Outreach sessions, Providers trained, GBV corners) | The programme's flagship SIDA results | **PHD 1 + PHD 2** (centres count comes from the centre list) |
| **Coverage map** | Where PHD works | Set up at the validation workshop |
| **Form activity grid** | How many of each kind of record came in this month | **PHD 1 + PHD 2** |
| **Indicator progress list** | Every PHD indicator, target vs. achieved | **PHD's forms** |

## 7. The Bandhu page — what you see and where it comes from

**The forms Bandhu field staff use:**
- **Bandhu 0 — Mother List** — registers each beneficiary once. Bandhu's master list.
- **Bandhu 1 — Service Log** — clinic visits, HIV testing, GBV support, counselling, referrals.
- **Bandhu 2 — Activity & Operations** — outreach, mobile camps, events/trainings, billboards.
- **No Reporting Today** — the nil-return form.

**What's on the page:**

| What you see | What it means | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **5 headline cards** (KP individuals served, HIV testing, GBV survivors supported, Drop-in Centres, Providers & leaders trained) | Bandhu's flagship UNFPA results | **Bandhu 1 + Bandhu 2** |

> **Remember:** Bandhu's numbers only count after UNFPA's approval (§4).

## 8. The CIPRB page — what you see and where it comes from

CIPRB's one page holds three monitoring areas: **Fistula**, **MPDSR** (death reviews), and **Maternal Near-Miss**.

**The forms CIPRB field staff use:**
- **CIPRB 1 — Fistula Question Bank** — follows each fistula patient through her journey: Suspected → Diagnosed → Referred → Operated → Rehabilitated.
- **CIPRB 2–5 — MPDSR Death Reviews** — community & facility reviews of maternal and newborn deaths.
- **CIPRB 6 — Social Autopsy** — the "three delays" review of a maternal death.
- **CIPRB 7 & 8 — Death Notification Slips** — the slips health workers send when a death occurs.
- **CIPRB 9 — Maternal Near-Miss** — women who survived a severe pregnancy complication.
- **CIPRB 10 — Response Plan** — the corrective actions agreed in review meetings, and their status.

**Fistula section:**

| What you see | What it means | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **"At a glance" band** | How many women are at each stage (Suspected → … → Rehabilitated) | **CIPRB 1** |
| **Reach & patient journey** | Districts covered and how women progress through the stages | **CIPRB 1** |
| **Fistula type (VVF / RVF)** | The anatomical type of fistula | **CIPRB 1** |
| **Surgical outcomes** | Operations that were successful (dry / not dry) vs. failed | **CIPRB 1** |
| **Diagnosis breakdown** | Cause of fistula (obstetric, etc.) | **CIPRB 1** |
| **17 detailed indicators** | Age, education, delivery history and more | **CIPRB 1** |

**MPDSR (death-review) section** — *all from the CIPRB MPDSR forms (2–8 & 10), plus district death estimates provided in Sayeed's spreadsheet:*

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Reporting rates** | How many deaths were reported and reviewed vs. the expected number |
| **Cause of death** | Why mothers / newborns died (community vs. facility) |
| **Facility deep-dive** | Time from admission to death, review progress, action-plan coverage |
| **Death notifications** | Deaths reported by kind, level and district |
| **Social autopsy** | The "three delays" findings |
| **11 detailed indicators** | Place/time of death, antenatal care, delivery details, etc. |
| **Response-plan tracker** | The agreed corrective actions and how many are done |

**Maternal Near-Miss panel** — survivors of severe complications, broken down by district, complication type and cause. *Comes from CIPRB 9.*

## 9. A few things to keep in mind when reading the numbers

- **An empty chart is not a fault.** It simply means no data has arrived yet for that item. Charts fill up as field staff submit.
- **Bandhu numbers wait for UNFPA.** They appear only after the second (UNFPA) approval.
- **The fistula "cause" breakdown fills later.** A woman's fistula *cause* is recorded when she reaches the operation stage, so that chart grows as cases progress — the *type* (VVF/RVF) and the stage counts fill earlier.
- **Every chart names its own source.** Look for the small grey **"Source"** tag in each chart's corner — it tells you exactly which form the number came from.

---

## 10. A short glossary

| Term | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **KoboToolbox** | The phone app field staff use to fill the forms |
| **Submission** | One completed form sent in from the field |
| **Approval** | A manager confirming a submission is correct so it can count |
| **Indicator** | A target the programme tracks (e.g. "FSWs reached") |
| **MPDSR** | Maternal & Perinatal Death Surveillance and Response — reviewing deaths to learn how to prevent them |
| **Fistula** | A childbirth injury; the programme finds, treats and rehabilitates women who have it |
| **Near-miss** | A woman who nearly died from a pregnancy complication but survived |
| **Drop-in Centre (DIC)** | A community centre where Bandhu delivers services |
| **FSW / KP** | Female sex worker / key population — the groups the programme serves |
