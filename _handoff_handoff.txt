9. Theory of Change
Derived from the SIDA Activity Frameworks for Bandhu (5 Districts, Key Populations) and PHD (11 Brothels, FSWs), and CIPRB (Fistula/Reproductive Health). This ToC defines the causal chain that the IDMS must track end-to-end.
9.1 Problem Statement
Key populations in Bangladesh — Female Sex Workers (FSWs), MSM, TG, MSW, and broader LGBTQI communities — bear a disproportionate burden of HIV/STI, GBV, and mental health conditions. Structural barriers including stigma, discrimination, criminalization, and lack of inclusive health services prevent them from accessing timely SRHR care. Fistula remains undetected and untreated among vulnerable women due to gaps in community screening and referral.
9.2 Causal Logic Chain
9.3 Theory of Change by Objective
Objective 1 — Service Delivery (Both Partners)
IF community-friendly service centers are established and FSWs/KP access HIV/STI screening, counseling, GBV support, and mental health services...
THEN early detection of HIV/STI increases, GBV survivors receive timely support, and SRHR service utilization improves among key populations.
BECAUSE accessible, stigma-free platforms remove structural barriers and trust is built through peer educators and outreach workers.
Objective 2 — Capacity Building (Both Partners)
IF government health managers, midwives, and peer educators are trained on inclusive, gender-affirmative care...
THEN service quality improves, discriminatory practices reduce, and referral pathways strengthen.
BECAUSE skills and awareness among frontline providers directly determine whether key populations receive dignified care.
Objective 3 / 4 — Community Awareness & IEC (PHD / Bandhu)
IF targeted IEC/SBCC materials and community messaging reach brothel areas and KP communities...
THEN stigma reduces, health-seeking behavior increases, and social accountability for rights violations improves.
BECAUSE sustained community-level messaging shifts norms and empowers KP to demand services.
CIPRB — Fistula & Reproductive Health
IF community campaigns identify suspected fistula cases and Fistula Corner diagnosis services are functional...
THEN confirmed fistula cases receive referral and treatment, and the baseline captures the unmet need.
BECAUSE systematic identification through campaigns + hospital corners creates a complete detection-to-treatment pipeline.
